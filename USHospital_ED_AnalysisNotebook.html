Hospital Clinical Quality & Emergency Department Performance Analysis¶

Project Overview¶

This project analyzes publicly available hospital-level performance data from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).

Business Objectives¶

  • Assess the completeness and consistency of hospital performance data.
  • Explore how records are distributed across states, conditions, and quality measures.
  • Analyze a selected emergency department measure(OP_18a).
  • Compare reported performance across states.
  • Identify unusual observations that may need further investigation.
  • Produce clean datasets and findings to support healthcare reporting.

Tools¶

  • Python
  • pandas
  • Matplotlib
  • Seaborn
  • Jupyter Notebook

Scope and Limitations¶

This analysis uses hospital reporting data. Statistical patterns and outliers are descriptive findings. They do not prove that a hospital provides better or worse care.

df.columns = df.columns.str.strip()

text_columns = [
    "Facility ID", "Facility Name", "Address", "City/Town",
    "State", "ZIP Code", "County/Parish", "Telephone Number",
    "Condition", "Measure ID", "Measure Name", "Score",
    "Sample", "Footnote"
]

for col in text_columns:
    df[col] = df[col].astype("string").str.strip()

# Convert empty strings into actual missing values
df[text_columns] = df[text_columns].replace(
    r"^\s*$", pd.NA, regex=True
)

# Standardize state codes
df["State"] = df["State"].str.upper()

# Convert scores to numeric where possible
df["Score"] = pd.to_numeric(df["Score"], errors="coerce")

# Convert sample size to numeric where possible
df["Sample"] = pd.to_numeric(df["Sample"], errors="coerce")

# Parse reporting dates
for col in ["Start Date", "End Date"]:
    df[col] = pd.to_datetime(
        df[col],
        format="%m/%d/%Y",
        errors="coerce"
    )

In [6]:
q1 = op18a_numeric["Score Numeric"].quantile(0.25)
q3 = op18a_numeric["Score Numeric"].quantile(0.75)

iqr = q3 - q1

lower_bound = q1 - 1.5 * iqr
upper_bound = q3 + 1.5 * iqr

op18a_numeric["Outlier"] = (
    (op18a_numeric["Score Numeric"] < lower_bound) |
    (op18a_numeric["Score Numeric"] > upper_bound)
)

print("Lower bound:", lower_bound)
print("Upper bound:", upper_bound)
print("Flagged records:", op18a_numeric["Outlier"].sum())

outliers = op18a_numeric.loc[
    op18a_numeric["Outlier"],

Key Findings¶

  • Identified 4,658 records for the OP_18a emergency department measure, including 4,052 records with numeric scores.
  • Calculated a median reported emergency department time of 154 minutes and a mean of approximately 162.8 minutes across numeric records.
  • Found that North Dakota had the lowest state-level median reported time at 109 minutes, followed by South Dakota at 119.5 minutes.
  • Flagged 66 potential outlier records using the IQR method for further investigation.
